## Research interests

## Research experience

### Robot learning & manipulation

**University of Arkansas · 2025–present**

Learning-based robotic manipulation in NVIDIA Isaac Lab, including UR10e simulation, teleoperation, multi-view observations, diffusion policies, and closed-loop evaluation.

### Surgical robotics research

**Purdue University · Summer 2025**

Research on learning from long-horizon egocentric surgical video and exploring language and vision models for transferring human demonstrations toward robotic execution on the da Vinci Research Kit.

### ChicGrasp: real-world imitation learning

**University of Arkansas · 2024–2025**

An autonomous manipulation system using a UR10e, a custom dual-jaw pneumatic gripper, multi-view RGB-D perception, and diffusion policies for irregular and deformable objects.

### Swarm manufacturing & robotic automation

**University of Arkansas · 2023**

Cooperative robotic manufacturing, SCARA integration, interchangeable end-effectors, and Python/C# tooling for converting manufacturing instructions into ABB RAPID.
