05 / ABOUT

Building intelligent robots for the physical world.

I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Arkansas working at the intersection of robot learning, robotic manipulation, imitation learning, simulation, and embodied AI.

My work focuses on learning-based manipulation systems that connect human demonstrations, multimodal observations, policy learning, simulation, and real-world robotic execution. I am especially interested in Diffusion Policy, generalist robot learning, Vision-Language-Action models, and scalable manipulation.
